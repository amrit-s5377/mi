# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three primary audiences — all on extended stays (1–4+ weeks) in Williston, ND:

1. **Bakken oilfield crews** — blue-collar workers on rotation, practical and value-focused; they need a reliable base with a kitchen so they can cook and save money
2. **Travel nurses** — professionals on 13-week contracts; they want comfort, consistency, and a quiet place to decompress after long hospital shifts
3. **Business travelers and corporate accounts** — contractors, energy-sector managers, and field reps working the Bakken; they need reliable Wi-Fi, a workspace, and professional amenities

All three share one job-to-be-done: find a place that feels like a temporary home, not a transient room.

## Product Purpose

Drive direct bookings for Microtel Inn & Suites by Wyndham Williston, ND. The site showcases kitchenette suites and extended-stay value over the generic chain alternatives in the area. Success means a visitor calls the front desk, fills the enquiry form, or clicks through to Wyndham's booking engine.

## Brand Personality

Comfortable, professional, welcoming. Grounded — not luxury, not flashy. The kind of hotel that earns loyalty through reliability, not aesthetics. Warm without being fussy; polished without being corporate.

## Anti-references

No strong opinions stated. Implied: avoid the sterile corporate chain aesthetic (the vanilla Hilton/Marriott brand site look) — this hotel has a specific local audience and should feel more human and direct than a global chain template.

## Design Principles

1. **Function first, feeling second** — every design choice earns its place by serving the guest's actual decision (should I stay here?), not by looking impressive
2. **Warmth through specificity** — call out real people (oilfield crew, travel nurse) rather than generic "guests"; specificity is the warmth
3. **Direct over decorative** — the call to action is always one step away; no dead ends, no buried phone numbers
4. **Honest confidence** — this is a Microtel, not a Marriott; own that positioning clearly rather than imitating luxury
5. **Local over generic** — Williston, ND has a specific character (energy industry town, Great Plains); the site should feel grounded in place, not could-be-anywhere

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. Key considerations: extended-stay guests often book on mobile in field conditions (bright sun, tired eyes) — contrast must be solid. No known specific user needs beyond standard AA compliance.
