Everything at a Glance

Free Wi-Fi

High-speed Wi-Fi throughout the property. No password at check-in, no daily charge, no speed throttling.

Free Parking

On-site parking at no charge. Spaces for standard vehicles, pickup trucks, heavy equipment trucks, and oversized vehicles.

24-Hour Front Desk

Staff on-site around the clock. Early check-in, late check-out, and any-hour arrivals accommodated for crew block accounts.

Pet-Friendly Rooms

Domestic pets welcome with advance notice. Designated pet-friendly rooms. Additional fee applies — please notify us at booking.

65" Smart TV

Every room has a 65-inch flat-screen TV with cable channels. Stream your own apps or watch live sports after a shift.

In-Room Safe

Every suite includes an in-room safe for securing valuables, passports, and documents during your stay.

Wyndham Rewards

Earn Wyndham Rewards points on every stay. Book via Wyndham.com or provide your number at check-in to ensure points are credited.

Full-Kitchen Suites

All 39 renovated suites feature a 2-burner cooktop, dishwasher, full fridge, microwave, coffee maker, and complete cookware kit.

Work Desk & Chair

Full-size work desk and ergonomic chair in every room. Designed for guests who work from the room on long stays.

Housekeeping

Daily housekeeping standard for all stays. Weekly housekeeping option available for extended-stay guests who prefer privacy.

Business Centre

Computer station and printer available in the lobby for printing confirmations, forms, and work documents.

Vending & Ice

Vending machines with snacks and beverages available around the clock. Ice machines on every floor.
