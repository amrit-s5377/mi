Built for the Bakken

The Man Camp Alternative — With a Full Kitchen

You've worked the rig. You've driven the truck. You've finished the shift. The last thing you want is a hotel room that's smaller than your truck cab and a vending machine for dinner.

Microtel Inn & Suites Williston has redesigned 39 of our suites for the way Bakken workers actually live. Full kitchens. Full-size beds. Free laundry. 24-hour front desk for shift changes. Quiet, secure, and 16 miles from Williston Basin International Airport.

What's Different

A Real Kitchen. Not Just a Microwave.

Each Queen Kitchenette Suite is set up like a small apartment. You get a 2-burner cooktop, a real dishwasher (not a sink full of dishes after every meal), a full-size refrigerator with a freezer for groceries from Walmart, a microwave with a vent hood, and a coffee maker that's ready when your alarm goes off at 4am.

We stock every suite with the cookware, knives, plates, mugs, and cutlery you need to actually cook a meal — not just heat up a frozen one. If you need a rice cooker, slow cooker, or air fryer, we keep extras at the front desk that you can borrow free for any stay over a week.

In winter, ask about our free boot dryers. We get it.
