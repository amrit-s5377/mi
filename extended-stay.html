Why Extended Stay?

Stay Longer. Pay Less. Live Better.

If you're working a Bakken contract, on a 13-week travel nurse assignment, relocating to Williston, or here for a long-term project — paying transient hotel rates makes no sense. Microtel Williston offers significantly lower weekly and monthly rates on our 39 newly renovated Queen Kitchenette Suites, designed for guests who stay a week or more.

Every suite is set up like a small apartment. You get a 2-burner cooktop, a real dishwasher, a full-size refrigerator with a freezer, a microwave with a vent hood, and a coffee maker. We stock every suite with cookware, knives, plates, mugs, and cutlery so you can actually cook — not just reheat.

Common Questions

Extended Stay FAQ

How do I get the weekly or monthly rate?

Call us directly at (701) 572-2000 or email us. We'll confirm your stay length and apply the discounted rate. You can also book through Wyndham.com and note your stay length — we'll match you to the right rate plan.

Do you accept direct corporate billing?

Yes. We accept direct billing for major oil & gas operators and travel nurse agencies including AMN Healthcare, Aya Healthcare, and Cross Country. Call us to set up a corporate account before your first booking.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes. We welcome domestic dogs and cats. An additional pet fee applies. Please notify us at the time of booking so we can assign a pet-friendly room.

How does housekeeping work for long stays?

For stays of 7+ nights, you can choose daily housekeeping or weekly housekeeping for privacy. Monthly guests default to weekly housekeeping with linen changes. Let us know your preference at check-in.

Can I book a block of rooms for my crew?

Absolutely. Call us directly at (701) 572-2000 for crew blocks of 5+ rooms. We'll work with your scheduling — early check-in, staggered check-out, multi-room blocks, and monthly rates that beat OTA pricing.

Is breakfast really free?

Yes. Every guest — at every rate plan — gets complimentary hot breakfast daily. Eggs, waffles, pastries, juice, fresh fruit, and coffee. No charge, no voucher needed.
