Lake Sakakawea

Year-round fishing on one of the largest man-made lakes in the US. Boating, kayaking & stunning prairie sunsets.

Walk the grounds where Sitting Bull surrendered in 1881. One of North Dakota's most significant historic landmarks.

The North Dakota Badlands in all their glory. Bison, wild horses, and dramatic canyon landscapes.

Overview

About the Property

Microtel Inn & Suites by Wyndham Williston is a modern 3-star hotel located at 3820 4th Ave West, Williston, North Dakota — just off Highway 85 and approximately 1 mile from Williston Basin International Airport (XWA).

Part of the global Wyndham Hotels & Resorts family, the property participates in the Wyndham Rewards programme, allowing guests to earn and redeem points on every stay. The hotel serves the northwest North Dakota region including Williston, Tioga, and the broader Bakken Formation area.

With 77 smartly designed guest rooms, an indoor heated pool, complimentary hot breakfast, and dedicated business facilities, Microtel Williston is the ideal base for both short-term leisure visits and extended corporate stays.
