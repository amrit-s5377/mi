Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is parking free?
Yes — complimentary on-site parking for all guests including spaces for trucks, buses, and oversized vehicles. No reservations needed.
Is breakfast included with every stay?
Yes. A free hot breakfast buffet is served every morning — eggs, waffles, pastries, juice and fresh fruit. No extra charge.
Do you have a pool?
Yes, an indoor heated pool and hot tub are available daily for all registered guests at no extra charge.
Are pets allowed?
Yes, well-behaved pets are welcome. An additional fee applies — please let us know when booking so we can prepare the right room.
What are the check-in and check-out times?
Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Our 24-hour front desk can often accommodate early arrivals based on availability.
Is Wi-Fi free?
Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi is included for every guest throughout all rooms and common areas — no password, no extra charge.
Do you offer weekly or monthly rates?
Yes. Guests staying 7+ nights receive discounted weekly rates, and monthly pricing is available for long-term stays. Contact us for a custom quote.
Do you have a meeting room?
Yes — an 800 sq ft meeting room seats up to 40 guests with AV equipment and catering on request. Ideal for team briefings and corporate training.
Can I earn loyalty points?
Yes, as a Wyndham Hotels & Resorts property you earn Wyndham Rewards points on every stay. Book on Wyndham.com to ensure points are credited.
Is laundry available on-site?
Yes — free guest laundry with industrial washers and dryers is available on-site. Perfect for extended stays and crew rotations.
