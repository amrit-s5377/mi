Room Overview

Real Kitchen. Real Cookware. Real Comfort.

Our newly renovated Queen Kitchenette Suite is built for guests who need more than a hotel room. Whether you're staying a night, a week, or a month, this suite gives you the comfort and equipment of a furnished apartment — with the service of a hotel.

Every Kitchenette Suite features a complete in-room kitchen with a 2-burner cooktop, full-size refrigerator-freezer, stainless dishwasher, over-the-range microwave with exhaust hood, drip coffee maker, and stainless electric kettle. The quartz prep counter and single-bowl undermount sink give you real cooking space, not just a drink station.

Each suite is stocked with everything you need to prepare and serve a meal: cookware, chef's knife and paring knife in a wooden block, cutting board, ceramic dinner plates and bowls, oversized coffee mugs, tempered drinking glasses, stemless wine glasses, full stainless cutlery sets, and prep tools including a colander, mixing bowls, vegetable peeler, can opener, and bottle opener. Coffee filters, dishwasher pods, salt & pepper, and other essentials are stocked at check-in.

Common Questions

Extended Stay FAQ

How do I get the weekly or monthly rate?

Call us directly at (701) 572-2000 or email us. We'll confirm your stay length and apply the discounted rate. You can also book through Wyndham.com and note your stay length — we'll match you to the right rate plan.

Do you accept direct corporate billing?

Yes. We accept direct billing for major oil & gas operators and travel nurse agencies including AMN Healthcare, Aya Healthcare, and Cross Country. Call us to set up a corporate account before your first booking.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes. We welcome domestic dogs and cats. An additional pet fee applies. Please notify us at the time of booking so we can assign a pet-friendly room. Please do not leave pets unattended in rooms for extended periods.

How does housekeeping work for long stays?

For stays of 7+ nights, you can choose daily housekeeping or weekly housekeeping for privacy. Monthly guests default to weekly housekeeping with linen changes. Let us know your preference at check-in.

Can I book a block of rooms for my crew?

Absolutely. Call us directly at (701) 572-2000 for crew blocks of 5+ rooms. We'll work with your scheduling — early check-in, staggered check-out, multi-room blocks, and monthly rates that beat OTA pricing.

Is breakfast really free?

Yes. Every guest — at every rate plan — gets complimentary hot breakfast daily. Eggs, waffles, pastries, juice, fresh fruit, and coffee. No charge, no voucher needed.
