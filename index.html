Full-Kitchen Suites

Welcome to Microtel Inn & Suites by Wyndham Williston — your home base in the heart of the Bakken oilfield region. We've recently completed a major suite renovation: 39 of our rooms are now full-kitchen Queen Suites, purpose-built for guests staying multiple nights, weeks, or months in Williston, North Dakota.

Located off Highway 85, just 16 miles from Williston Basin International Airport (XWA) and 0.4 miles from CHI St. Alexius Health Williston Medical Center. Minutes from Halliburton, Schlumberger, and other major Bakken operators.

Explore Williston

From ancient history to wide-open prairie adventures — Williston has more to offer than you think.

3 miles

Lake Sakakawea

Year-round fishing on one of the largest man-made lakes in the US. Boating, kayaking & stunning prairie sunsets.

24 miles

Fort Union Trading Post

Historic fur-trade fort at the confluence of the Missouri and Yellowstone rivers. Walk where history was made.

60 miles

Theodore Roosevelt National Park

The North Dakota Badlands in all their glory. Bison, wild horses, and dramatic canyon landscapes.

Williston's premier recreation centre. Swimming, sports, fitness, and family activities year-round.
