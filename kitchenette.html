Room Overview

Real Kitchen. Real Cookware. Real Comfort.

Our newly renovated Queen Kitchenette Suite is built for guests who need more than a hotel room. Whether you're staying a night, a week, or a month, this suite gives you the comfort and equipment of a furnished apartment — with the service of a hotel.

Every Kitchenette Suite features a complete in-room kitchen with a 2-burner cooktop, full-size refrigerator-freezer, stainless dishwasher, over-the-range microwave with exhaust hood, drip coffee maker, and stainless electric kettle. The quartz prep counter and single-bowl undermount sink give you real cooking space, not just a drink station.

Each suite is stocked with everything you need to prepare and serve a meal: cookware, chef's knife and paring knife in a wooden block, cutting board, ceramic dinner plates and bowls, oversized coffee mugs, tempered drinking glasses, stemless wine glasses, full stainless cutlery sets, and prep tools including a colander, mixing bowls, vegetable peeler, can opener, and bottle opener. Coffee filters, dishwasher pods, salt & pepper, and other essentials are stocked at check-in.
