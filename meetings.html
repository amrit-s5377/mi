Room Features

Everything Set Up — Before You Arrive

Projector & Screen

Full HD projector and retractable screen. HDMI and VGA connections for laptops. Screen fills the end wall — visible from every seat.

Microphone & Audio

Handheld and lapel microphone available. PA speaker system for presentations, briefings, and training sessions of all sizes.

Dedicated Wi-Fi

Separate high-speed Wi-Fi network for meeting room attendees. Handles video calls, presentations, and multiple simultaneous connections.

Whiteboard & Markers

Full-width whiteboard at the front of the room. Markers, erasers, and flip chart pads available for workshop-style sessions.

Flexible Seating

Reconfigurable tables and chairs. Boardroom (14–16), classroom (24–28), theatre (40), or U-shape (20) layouts available on request.

Catering Service

Breakfast trays, coffee & tea service, lunch platters, and afternoon snack arrangements available with 24-hour notice. Priced per head.
