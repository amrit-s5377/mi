About the Property

Microtel Inn & Suites by Wyndham Williston is a modern 3-star hotel at 3820 4th Ave West, Williston, North Dakota — just off Highway 85 and 16 miles from Williston Basin International Airport (XWA). We've recently completed a major renovation of 39 suites into full-kitchen extended-stay rooms — the only hotel in Williston offering this level of in-room kitchen equipment.

Part of the global Wyndham Hotels & Resorts family, the property participates in the Wyndham Rewards programme. The hotel serves the northwest North Dakota region including Williston, Tioga, and the broader Bakken Formation area — with dedicated facilities for corporate, oilfield workforce, and travel medical guests.

Whether you're a contract worker on a 21/7 rotation, a travel medical professional on assignment, or a family relocating to Williston, our renovated suites give you the comfort of home with the service of a hotel.
